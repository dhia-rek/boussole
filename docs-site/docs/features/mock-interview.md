## What it is

**Mock interview** (job detail → **Mock interview**) runs a practice interview for one job. An AI interviewer, built from the job description and your CV, asks questions, follows up when an answer stays vague, and ends with a scored report: overall score, recommendation, scores per competency, strengths and what to improve.

## Why it exists

Interviews for AI, data and automation roles quickly go into specifics ("what exactly triggers the human validation?"). Practicing on the real job, with follow-ups on your own projects, shows where your answers stay too general before a recruiter does.

## How to use it

1. Open a job in **Jobs** and click **Mock interview**. When an application reaches an interview stage, **Today** also shows "Prepare your … with …": **Practice now** opens the interview for that job directly.
2. Choose the round: **HR / Behavioral**, **Technical**, or **Full loop**.
3. Optionally pick the **Interviewer voice** and click **Preview**. Boussole picks the most natural voice your computer offers for the interview's language and never picks novelty voices (for example macOS "Grandpa").
4. Click **Start interview**.
5. Answer each question by typing, or with **Answer by voice** (your speech is transcribed in the interview's language). Answering by voice interrupts the interviewer.
6. The interview ends by itself, or click **Finish and get feedback** at any time after your first answer.

Camera and microphone are optional. Without them, you type your answers and everything else works the same. The speaker button turns the interviewer voice off or on (remembered on this browser).

The interview is in the language of the job: French for a French posting, English otherwise.

## Common problems

- **The voice sounds robotic.** Pick another voice under **Interviewer voice**. On macOS, better French voices (for example "Audrey (Premium)") can be downloaded in System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices.
- **No voice at all.** The speaker button may be off, or the browser has no voice for that language.
- **"Answer by voice" is missing.** Your browser doesn't support speech recognition (for example Firefox). Type your answers.
- **"End" vs "Finish and get feedback".** **End** closes without a report; **Finish and get feedback** scores what you answered so far.
