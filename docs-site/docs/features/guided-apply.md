## What it is

Two pages that take you from "which job?" to "applied", one decision at a time:

- **Choose your next application** (`/review`): your good matches, one at a time.
- **Apply step by step** (`/apply/<job id>`): one application in five short steps, one screen each.

## Why it exists

The job page shows everything at once: brief, CV check, research, Ghostwriter, notes and timeline. That helps when you know what you want, and overwhelms when you don't. The guided pages show only what the current step needs and remember where you stopped.

## How to use it

### Choose your next application

1. On **Today**, click **Review them** on "Choose your next application".
2. Read the job: the role, why it matches you, and its score. **Read the whole job** opens the full job page.
3. Choose:
   - **Apply to this one**: opens the guided application at the CV step.
   - **Not for me**: moves the job to Skipped. It is not suggested again.
   - **Decide later**: shows the next job and keeps this one for next time.

Only jobs that score 60 or more with a real AI score (not a keyword estimate), were found in the last 45 days, and haven't been started are listed, best first (up to 50).

### Apply step by step

Open it from **Choose your next application**, from **Today** ("Finish your application to …"), or with **Apply step by step** on a job that is New or Ready (job page Overview, or the job panel in **Jobs**).

1. **The job**: the match score and the job summary. **Not for me** skips the job and goes back to Today.
2. **Your CV**: the [CV check](/docs/features/cv-check) (which CV lines fit, which to reword, which to cut) and the CV you will send. For a job without a tailored CV yet, **Prepare my CV for this job** makes one (about a minute). Your saved CV is never changed.
3. **The company**: [company research](/docs/features/company-research) with a source for every fact.
4. **Your message**: [Ghostwriter](/docs/features/ghostwriter) writes the application email when the posting says to apply by email, otherwise a cover letter. **A cover letter instead** / **An email instead** switches. Ask for changes in the chat.
5. **Send it**: how to apply (email, LinkedIn, the company website…). After applying, click **Yes, mark applied**. Boussole shows the date it will remind you to follow up (7 days later).

**Next** and **Back** move between steps; click a step to jump to it. A check mark shows the steps already done for this job. Opened without `?step=`, the page starts at the first step still to do.

## Common problems

### "Prepare my CV for this job" or the message step fails

Both use your AI account. Check **Settings → AI account** and sign in again if needed. See [AI Account](/docs/features/ai-account).

### "No good new matches right now"

No recent job scored 60 or more with a real AI score. Run a search (**Search for more jobs**), or on **Today** use **Recalculate now** if your jobs only have keyword estimates.

### A step has no check mark although I did it

Check marks refresh when you change step. A step counts as done when its result exists: a finished CV check, finished company research, a Ghostwriter conversation for this job, or the job marked as applied.
